# Study progress, teacher Q&A and bilingual interface

- Login introduction: “All you need to 5** in DSE Physics”.
- The 192 bilingual 2012–2014 Paper 2 MC records show their verified answer letter. Original marking scans remain retained as provenance.
- E1.1.1 displays “天体系统的层级与尺度”, with a concise bilingual explanation. Related-question panels display the knowledge title without internal evidence notes.
- English mode covers student navigation, account controls, study content, print labels, status messages and the administrator interface. User-written questions and replies retain their original language.
- Question mastery and mistakes are mutually exclusive and stored per account. Paper 1A, 1B and 2 support whole-paper selection and atomic batch updates. Restoring a mistake clears its mastery state.
- Students can ask about an exact question, knowledge point, or general study issue. The dashboard's Q&A section shows student identity and exact context, and sends private replies visible on the student's Q&A page.

The existing SQLite database receives additive `mastered` and `tutoring_questions` tables on startup. Existing accounts, membership, mistakes and sessions are preserved.

Validation: 61 automated tests; 2,232 bilingual question records audited; 820 bilingual knowledge records audited; desktop/mobile English UI checks; exact question/knowledge selection and teacher-to-student reply flow tested locally.

The requested Chinese Word conversions are delivered separately as 43 watermarked PDFs (1,204 pages). These documents are not included in the public download catalog.
